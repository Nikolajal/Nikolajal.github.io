<div class="lang-block" data-lang="en" markdown="1">

I'm a postdoc at **INFN Bologna**, working on detector R&D for the [ePIC experiment](https://www.epic-eic.org/) at the future Electron-Ion Collider. My day usually splits between writing C++ that turns raw silicon photomultiplier signals into physics measurements, and being on the floor during beam tests making sure the hardware actually does what it's supposed to.

I came up through **ALICE at CERN** during my PhD, and most of how I think about data, uncertainties, and detectors was shaped there. Now I lead the analysis framework for the **dRICH Cherenkov prototype** — from raw SiPM data all the way through to the numbers that get presented to the collaboration — and I'm the **analysis coordinator** for its beam-test campaigns at CERN, having also helped commission the hardware itself. Since 2025 I also chair the EIC User Group's **Elections & Nominations Committee**.

Outside the lab I tutor physics — high school through university — and I'm involved with a few civic organisations in Bologna. I hold a PhD in Particle Physics from the University of Bologna (2024), an MSc *cum laude* in Physics, and I have both Italian and French citizenship.

If you want the long version, the [full CV is right here](/cv/).
