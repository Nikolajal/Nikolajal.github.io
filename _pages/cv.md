<h2>
  <span class="lang-block" data-lang="en">Profile</span>
  <span class="lang-block" data-lang="it" hidden>Profilo</span>
  <span class="lang-block" data-lang="fr" hidden>Profil</span>
</h2>

<div class="lang-block" data-lang="en" markdown="1">
Experimental particle physicist specialising in the development and characterisation of silicon-photomultiplier (SiPM) photodetectors. Since 2020 I have worked on single-photon Cherenkov imaging for the particle identification (PID) of the dual-radiator RICH (dRICH) of ePIC at the Electron-Ion Collider — with the Bologna group I assembled, tested and brought into operation the 2048-channel SiPM readout plane of the dRICH prototype, and through the analysis of four CERN beam tests demonstrated its operability at −30 °C in beam. I pair research with ongoing teaching and outreach.
</div>
<div class="lang-block" data-lang="it" hidden markdown="1">
Fisico delle particelle sperimentale, specializzato nello sviluppo e nella caratterizzazione di fotorivelatori al silicio (SiPM). Dal 2020 lavoro all'imaging di singoli fotoni Cherenkov per la particle identification (PID) del dual-radiator RICH (dRICH) di ePIC all'Electron-Ion Collider: assieme al gruppo di Bologna ho assemblato, testato e portato in operazione il piano di fotorivelazione a SiPM (2048 canali) del prototipo dRICH e, analizzando i dati di quattro beam test al CERN, ne ho dimostrato la fattibilità operativa a −30 °C su fascio. Affianco alla ricerca un'attività didattica e di terza missione già in corso.
</div>
<div class="lang-block" data-lang="fr" hidden markdown="1">
Physicien des particules expérimental, spécialisé dans le développement et la caractérisation de photodétecteurs au silicium (SiPM). Depuis 2020, je travaille sur l'imagerie de photons Cherenkov uniques pour l'identification des particules (PID) du dual-radiator RICH (dRICH) d'ePIC à l'Electron-Ion Collider : avec le groupe de Bologne, j'ai assemblé, testé et mis en opération le plan de photodétection à SiPM (2048 canaux) du prototype dRICH et, en analysant les données de quatre tests sur faisceau au CERN, j'en ai démontré l'exploitabilité à −30 °C sous faisceau. J'associe à la recherche une activité d'enseignement et de médiation scientifique déjà en cours.
</div>

<div class="lang-block" data-lang="en" markdown="1">
**Postdoctoral Researcher** · INFN Bologna · Nov 2023 – Present · *by competition*
* Lead the analysis framework for the ePIC dRICH Cherenkov prototype — from raw SiPM data through to collaboration-approved results — and serve as **beam-test analysis coordinator**.
* Built and maintain the SiPM characterisation lab (IV, DCR, PDE, timing before/after irradiation and annealing) and its largely automated measurement + analysis pipeline, still in use at INFN Bologna.

* At the CERN PS/SPS beam tests: validated the ALCOR-based acquisition chain, demonstrated operability of a high-density SiPM box at −30 °C, and scaled the prototype to 2048 channels; contributed to slow control, assembly, and an online analysis/QA GUI in Qt.

**Doctoral Researcher** · University of Bologna & ALICE Collaboration (CERN) · Nov 2020 – Mar 2024 · *by competition*
* Developed and brought to **ALICE collaboration approval** a measurement of **φ-meson pair production** — a probe of strangeness production — extended across energies and differentiated in multiplicity; showed the data favour the Lund string model over the thermal model (publication in preparation).
* **Visiting researcher, Lund University** (Oct–Dec 2022): a rapidity-correlation measurement distinguishing string-based from statistical production.
* ALICE detector operations: **on-call TOF expert** (2021–22) and **Shift Leader** (spring 2023); reviewed two analyses for the **Analysis Review Committee**.
